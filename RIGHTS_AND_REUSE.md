The repository contains original project descriptions, working coding notes, source links and verification utilities. Its public visibility alone does **not** establish an open-source or open-data licence.

- Linked agency notices, press articles, standards, names and third-party content remain under their respective rights and terms.
- Source excerpts are paraphrased to avoid redistributing entire articles or source PDFs.
- No third-party rights are granted by this repository.
- The project author should choose an explicit licence for the author's own code, original documentation and derived tables only after reviewing any institutional requirements and rights in underlying sources.
- No actual candidate records, private bank details, unpublished third-party datasets or credentials should be uploaded.

Contact the repository owner before reproducing or modifying materials beyond applicable law or licences.
